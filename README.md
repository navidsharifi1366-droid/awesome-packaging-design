# Awesome Packaging Design

A free open-source collection of professional packaging design resources, AI prompts, mockups, FMCG product photography references, dielines, and print-production guidelines.

## What You'll Find Here

### AI Packaging Prompts
Professional prompts for:
- FMCG product hero shots
- Product photography
- Luxury packaging
- Tea & supplement packaging
- Cosmetic packaging
- Print-ready mockups

### Packaging Guidelines
- Print production tips
- CMYK & color management
- Packaging mistakes to avoid
- Typography for packaging
- Label hierarchy

### Mockups & Resources
- Packaging mockup references
- Dieline resources
- Print preparation workflow

## Goal
Helping packaging designers, brand owners, and AI creators build better packaging faster.
